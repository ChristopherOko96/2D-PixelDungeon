# Decor tiles for the Dungeon-Werkstatt — source and license note

Cut unchanged (16 px art, drawn at 2x) from two packs on the owner's desktop
(`Sprite Sheet 0x72`), 2026-10-28:

- **DungeonTileset II Extended v1.1** (`dungeontileset-extended.png`) by
  Nijikokun, https://nijikokun.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii-extended, an extension
  of 0x72's DungeonTileset II. The itch.io listing calls the pack free to use
  under **CC0**; the download ships no license file. Taken from it:
  `carpet_red`, `carpet_beige` (each a 3x3 sheet of the pack's carpet corner,
  edge and middle tiles, rearranged), `rug_red`, `rug_beige`, `floor_crack_1-5`,
  `floor_dark_1-2`, `floor_moss`, `wall_ring`, `wall_grate`, `wall_crack_1-2`,
  `sign_1-4`, `signpost_1-2`, `cabinet`, `skull_small`, `fire_f0-7`.
  The candle columns and wall torches in `../crypt_props/` come from the same pack.
- **DungeonTileset II v1.7** by 0x72 (CC0, see `../0x72/LICENSE-NOTE.md`):
  `wall_goo`, `wall_goo_base`.

The catalog that names, sorts and places these tiles is
`assets/data/dungeon_decor.json`. If either author ever publishes different
terms, re-check them here.
