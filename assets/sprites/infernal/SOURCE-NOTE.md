# Infernal creatures and Molgrax

`source/monsters_sheet.webp` (8 creatures) and `source/boss_sheet.webp`
(Molgrax) were provided by the project owner on 2026-10-26 (generated sprite
sheets). `scripts/slice_infernal_sheets.py` cuts them into the frames in this
folder (background removed by flood fill, aligned on the feet, 1 px outline).
Re-run the script after replacing a sheet.

The owner is responsible for the usage rights of the source images.
