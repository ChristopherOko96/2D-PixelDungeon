# Forest companions (bear, wolf)

Source: "Enchanted Forest Characters" by Superdark, made as a companion pack to
0x72's DungeonTileset II — https://superdark.itch.io/enchanted-forest-characters
(the user's art archive `Sprite Sheet 0x72.rar`, folder
`Enchanted Forest - Individual Frames/Bear` and `/Wolf`).

License (itch.io page, checked 29.10.2026): free, usable in free and commercial
projects, sprites may be modified, no credit required.

Files: `bear_idle_f0..3`, `bear_walk_f0..3`, `wolf_idle_f0..3`, `wolf_walk_f0..3`
(32x32, renamed from `Bear_Idle_1.png` etc., pixels unchanged). Used for the
beast master's companion (src/vfx/talent_fx.gd).
